# Pricing

## Accessible Learning Infrastructure

Quazom is built around the belief that meaningful, structured learning should be accessible.

Traditional education can be prohibitively expensive. Many online learning platforms also lock important features behind aggressive pricing tiers or prioritize engagement metrics over actual understanding.

Quazom aims to provide a different experience:
- affordable access
- transparent pricing
- flexible learning
- long-term intellectual growth

The goal is not to charge hundreds or thousands of dollars for access to information. The goal is to create a sustainable platform that helps people build their own systems of learning and research over time.

---

# Plans

## Explorer — $5/month or $100/year

For curious learners exploring new subjects and building study habits.

### Includes
- 10 curriculum generations per month
- 100 lesson generations per month
- Study scheduling
- Markdown note-taking
- Annotations
- Progression tracking
- Module graduation system
- Resource uploads
- Continuity Notes
- Note importing/exporting

### Best For
- casual learners
- hobbyists
- students supplementing coursework
- lifelong learners exploring multiple interests

### Philosophy

Explorer is intentionally priced to remain accessible.

The goal is to lower the barrier to structured learning while still allowing users to meaningfully explore the platform and build real learning systems around their interests.

---

## Scholar — $15/month or $150/year

For deep learners, researchers, polymaths, and users building long-term educational systems inside Quazom.

### Includes
Everything in Explorer, plus:
- Unlimited curriculum generation
- Unlimited lesson generation
- Priority access to new features
- Advanced Continuity Curricula
- Expanded interdisciplinary generation
- Future research-focused tools and workflows

### Best For
- independent researchers
- writers
- creators
- graduate students
- intellectually curious professionals
- interdisciplinary learners
- heavy Quazom users

### Philosophy

Scholar is designed for users who want Quazom to become part of their long-term intellectual workflow.

As Quazom evolves, Scholar users will gain access to deeper research tools, continuity systems, and advanced learning infrastructure designed for sustained knowledge development over time.

---

# Future Plans

As Quazom grows, additional features may eventually become available, including:
- collaborative/shared curricula
- research mode
- knowledge graphs
- adaptive curricula
- scholarly profiles
- archival exports
- source-aware research systems

The focus will remain on building meaningful educational tools rather than fragmenting the platform into excessive pricing tiers.

---

# A Note on Pricing

Quazom is still early in development, and pricing may evolve over time as features expand and infrastructure costs become clearer.

However, the long-term goal is to keep Quazom significantly more accessible than traditional educational pathways while still supporting the sustainability of the platform.

Learning should not feel financially out of reach.

Quazom exists to help people explore, study, research, and grow; not to gate curiosity behind unnecessary complexity or pricing barriers.
