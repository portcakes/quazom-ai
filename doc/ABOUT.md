# About Quazom

## Build Your Own University

Quazom is an AI-powered platform for self-directed learning, interdisciplinary research, and long-term intellectual growth.

The traditional education system works well for some people, but many learners fall through the cracks. Some people want to study outside rigid degree structures. Some want to pursue multiple interests at once. Some learn best through exploration, experimentation, and connected thinking rather than standardized pathways.

Quazom exists for those people.

At its core, Quazom is designed to help people build their own evolving systems of learning; systems that adapt to their interests, goals, pace, and curiosity over time.

Rather than replacing learning with AI, Quazom is built around the idea that AI can help people structure, organize, and deepen their learning journeys. The goal is not passive consumption, but active exploration and intellectual growth.

Quazom combines curriculum generation, lesson generation, note-taking, progression systems, scheduling tools, and continuity-based learning into a single platform designed to feel less like a productivity app and more like a personal university.

---

# The Mission

Quazom’s mission is to make structured, meaningful learning more accessible, flexible, and personalized.

The internet contains an almost infinite amount of information, but information alone does not create understanding. Many people struggle not because they lack intelligence or curiosity, but because they lack:
- structure
- direction
- continuity
- guidance
- organization
- confidence in where to begin

Quazom aims to bridge that gap.

The long-term vision is to create a platform where users can:
- explore subjects deeply
- connect ideas across disciplines
- build long-term bodies of knowledge
- archive and revisit their thinking
- generate evolving research paths
- document intellectual growth over time

Quazom is not designed to replace schools or universities. Instead, it is designed to complement and expand the ways people learn independently, creatively, and continuously throughout their lives.

# Current Features

## Curriculum Generation

Generate personalized curricula tailored to a subject, skill level, learning goals, and areas of interest.

Quazom structures learning into modules and lessons, creating guided educational pathways that feel closer to a university course or self-directed study program than a simple AI chat.

---

## Lesson Generation

Generate individual lessons on demand, including:
- readings
- explanations
- quizzes
- exercises
- projects
- guided discussions (skippable — Skip marks the lesson complete and counts toward curriculum progression)

Lessons are designed to be modular, allowing users to study at their own pace while expanding or refining topics as needed.

---

## Study Scheduling

Create flexible study schedules to help maintain consistency and structure.

Learning goals become significantly easier to sustain when supported by intentional planning and routine.

---

## Markdown Note-Taking

Write and organize notes using Markdown.

Quazom intentionally supports portable, flexible note formats rather than locking users into proprietary systems.

---

## Annotations

Highlight and annotate lessons, resources, and study materials directly within the platform.

Annotations help users actively engage with content instead of passively consuming information.

---

## Progression Tracking

Track progress through curricula, modules, and lessons.

Quazom is built around the idea that visible progress and long-term continuity are important parts of maintaining motivation and momentum.

---

## Module Graduation

Progress through structured learning stages:
- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced

As users complete modules and assessments, Quazom tracks advancement and mastery over time.

---

## Resource File Uploads

Upload PDFs, articles, research papers, and other learning resources directly into Quazom.

Future plans include:
- AI-assisted resource parsing
- quote extraction
- semantic search
- source-aware lesson integration

---

## Continuity Notes

Create persistent notes that span multiple lessons, modules, curricula, and research areas.

Continuity Notes are designed to function as long-term intellectual workspaces where users can continuously build upon ideas over time.

Users can write and edit in rich text, add links to lessons and resources, and add tags to organize their notes.

---

## Note Importing & Exporting

Import and export notes and research archives in PDF, TXT, or MD format.

Users should always maintain ownership and accessibility of their work.

---

## TTS Generation & Audio Library

Generate and store spoken audio versions of notes and resources in an audio library.

Users can listen to their notes and resources through the audio player.

## College catalog/academic resource links to curriculum generation [SHIPPED - May 27, 2026]

Generate curricula based on a specific college or university course catalog.

Users can submit a link to a college or university course catalog, and Quazom will generate a curriculum based on the courses in the catalog.

Users can submit links to specific classes or course overviews.

Users can submit links to academic websites, academic journals, online doctoral dissertations, court records, and other academic resources.

Users can also submit a link to a specific course syllabus.

Submitted links go through Quazom's reader-mode extractor synchronously before any AI tokens are spent, so a paywalled or unreachable URL is rejected at the form rather than producing a half-extracted curriculum. Social-media domains (YouTube, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X) are blocked outright.

---

## Uploaded Resources to curriculum generation [SHIPPED - May 27, 2026]

Generate curricula based on the resources uploaded by the user.

Users can upload PDFs of course syllabi, articles, research papers, and other learning resources directly into Quazom.

Quazom will generate a curriculum based on the resources uploaded. Uploads are capped at 50 MB per file (TXT / PDF / Markdown).

---

## Continuity Curricula [SHIPPED - May 27, 2026]

Generate interdisciplinary curricula informed by two or more sources — extra topics, uploaded files, saved links, and (optionally) a thesis statement synthesised from the user's continuity notes.

Rather than isolating subjects into separate silos, Continuity Curricula are designed to encourage connected thinking and intellectual exploration across disciplines.

Examples might include:
- architecture + sociology
- theology + ecology
- systems engineering + anthropology
- mathematics + music theory

Continuity Curricula count against a separate per-plan quota:
- FREE / ALPHA: 2 lifetime
- Explorer: 5 per month
- Scholar: 20 per month

Users can also fine-tune which lesson types (quizzes, exercises, projects, discussions, readings, other) Quazom is allowed to generate for a given curriculum, and the choice is respected when the curriculum is later extended to the next difficulty tier.

The `video` lesson type was retired in May 2026 because the AI cannot reliably embed real videos — the type had degraded into a YouTube-search placeholder that felt like filler. New curricula can no longer opt into Video lessons; existing curricula that already contain Video lessons keep generating and viewing them so no learner loses progress.

Discussion lessons are skippable. The chat box has a Skip button next to Send that marks the discussion as complete (with a distinct "Skipped" badge in place of "Complete") and counts it toward both curriculum progression % and the next-level unlock gate — so a learner who isn't in the mood to debate the prompt is never stuck on a single lesson.

# Philosophy

Quazom is built around a simple idea:

Learning should not end when school does.

Curiosity is not something that should be restricted to classrooms, semesters, or degrees. People should be able to explore ideas deeply, build personal systems of knowledge, and pursue learning throughout their lives in ways that feel meaningful to them.

Quazom exists to help make that possible.
